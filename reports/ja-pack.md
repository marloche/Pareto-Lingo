## Hand table (scripts/lib/stub-extras.ts)

- **私** → わたし: the standard word for "I"; あたし is a casual variant
- **何** → なに: the dictionary form; なん is the same word before d-, t- and n- sounds
- **人** → ひと: "person"; じん and にん are suffixes (日本人, 三人), never alone
- **君** → きみ: "you"; くん is the name suffix, written after a name, never alone
